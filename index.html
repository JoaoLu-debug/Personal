Manifesto

Exploring the tension between physical constraints and digital infinity. Translating algorithms into soundwaves, and light into tactile structures.

Phantom operates at the intersection of electronic music production and interactive design. Drawing inspiration from early club culture posters, modular synthesis, and glass optics, this archive represents a digital reflection of structural soundscapes.
